# 💫 About Me:
I am a Senior Full Stack Developer with over 6 years of experience building robust, scalable web and mobile applications. My expertise is centered on the .NET and Angular ecosystem, where I focus on writing clean, maintainable code that solves real-world business problems.<br><br>Throughout my career, I have had the opportunity to work on diverse and impactful projects, including:<br><br>• Enterprise Solutions: Developing core business modules for ERP systems and automated workflows for Timesheets and Purchase Orders.<br>• Public Safety: Building crime analytics dashboards and secure CCTV management portals for the US Police Departments.<br>• Integrations & Cloud: Seamlessly connecting applications with third-party services like Xero, Twilio, and Firebase, and managing deployments on AWS.<br><br>I thrive in Agile environments and enjoy the challenge of modernizing legacy systems or building new platforms from the ground up. My goal is always to balance technical excellence with business value, ensuring that every line of code contributes to a better user experience.<br><br>Technical Toolkit:<br>• Frontend: Angular, Angular Ionic, TypeScript, HTML/CSS<br>• Backend: ASP.NET Core, C#, Web API, Entity Framework, Dapper <br>• Databases: SQL Server, PostgreSQL <br>• Cloud & Tools: AWS (EC2, S3, RDS), Power BI, RESTful APIs <br><br>I am always open to connecting with fellow developers, tech enthusiasts, or potential collaborators.
